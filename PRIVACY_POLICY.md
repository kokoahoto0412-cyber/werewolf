## English Version

### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Sambung Kata (MitsuBot)** ("we," "our," or "the Bot"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users across all Discord servers and companion web mini-games (including *Mitsu Werewolf Web Edition*, *Draw & Guess*, and *Monopoly Negara Dunia*). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

### 2. Information We Collect
When you interact with the Bot on Discord or log into our companion web games, we collect only the minimum required data to provide and maintain our gaming services:
* **Discord Account Identifiers:** Your Discord User ID (`userId`), Username (`username`), and Guild (Server) ID (`guildId`).
* **Game Progress & Statistics:** In-game points, lifetime earnings, win counts, titles, achievements, inventory items, character inventory (`owned_chars`), awakening crystals, and Boss Raid scores stored locally inside our secure database (`mitsu_stats.db`).
* **Web Session Tokens:** Temporary, randomly generated session identifiers (`sessionId`) used solely to sync real-time game lobbies between Discord and our web browser interfaces.

### 3. Message Content & Privileged Intents
* **In-Memory Word Validation Only:** We use the Discord **Message Content Intent** strictly to operate real-time word-chain ("Sambung Kata") games inside designated Discord text channels. When an active game round is running, incoming message strings are evaluated in-memory against our Indonesian dictionary database (`indonesian_words.txt`) to verify word validity and compute typing reaction speed.
* **No Storage of Message Content:** **We DO NOT log, store, archive, or transmit your message content, chat history, or personal conversations to any database or external file system.** Once a message is evaluated for word-chain validation, its text string is immediately discarded from memory.
* **No AI/Machine Learning Training:** We never use any message content, chat data, or user communications to train machine learning models, artificial intelligence algorithms, or LLMs.

### 4. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data exclusively to:
* Track and display leaderboards, player points, and competitive standings across Discord servers.
* Manage user inventories, gacha character unlocks, and RPG progression.
* Authenticate host controls and secure game sessions against spoofing or unauthorized access.
* Prevent spam, cheating, and abuse of game systems.

### 5. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or external analytics services. All data is processed securely on our private servers.

### 6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
* **Retention:** Your basic game statistics (`userId`, `points`, `inventory`) are retained as long as the Bot is active to ensure your progression is saved across gaming sessions.
* **Right to Deletion (Opt-Out):** You have the right to request the complete deletion of your profile and data from our database at any time. To request data deletion, you or a server administrator can contact the Bot Owner directly via Discord or issue our data reset commands. Upon verification, all records associated with your `userId` will be permanently erased.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
